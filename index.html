The story

You slept through the end of the world

To live forever, you chose to have your body frozen.

While you slept, a deadly plague broke out, and humanity fell.

Months later the facility's power ran out and your pod opened. You remember nothing.

Now you have only one aim: to survive.

The game

How you stay alive

  • One day at a time

    Every real day brings a new day out there. Daylight runs from 06:00 to 20:00 in fifteen real minutes, and the clock keeps going while you stand still.

  • Anything you find is a weapon

    Fists, bats, axes, crowbars and guns. Every swing wears your weapon down, and every gunshot draws the dead in.

  • Loot every house

    Towns, farms, trailer parks, suburbs and roadside stops, upstairs and down. Search every cupboard: what you empty stays empty.

  • Eat, drink, sleep… or pass out

    Hunger, thirst and energy fall through the day, and stress builds up. Let one run out and you faint where you stand.

  • Bitten? Bind your wounds

    Bites and cuts keep bleeding until you bandage them. No bandage? Tear up a sheet.

  • Plough through the horde

    Abandoned cars still run if you find the petrol. Drive fast and you run the dead down, but the engine calls more of them.
