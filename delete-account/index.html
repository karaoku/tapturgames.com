Delete your Zomnia account

Zomnia by Taptur Games · Android and iOS

An account in Zomnia is optional. You have one only if you linked the game to Google or Apple in Settings → Account. Deleting it removes your account and the save kept in the cloud. You can do it in the game, or ask us by email.

In the game (immediate)

  1. Open Zomnia. You need an internet connection.
  2. On the main menu, tap Settings.
  3. Under Account, tap Delete account.
  4. Tap it again (Sure? Tap again) to confirm.

The game shows “Your account and cloud save are deleted.” This happens at once and cannot be undone. The game on this phone stays where it is; delete the app as well if you want that gone too.

By email

If you no longer have the game, email support@tapturgames.com with the subject “Delete my Zomnia account”, and include:

  • the email address of the Google or Apple account you signed in with, and which of the two it was;
  • if you used Apple’s “Hide My Email”, the relay address shown in your Apple Account settings under Sign in with Apple.

Please write from that address if you can; otherwise we may ask you to confirm that the account is yours. We delete the account within 30 days and reply when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your account: account ID, email address and sign-in service.
  • Your cloud save: the saved game, the day reached, last update time, save version and platform.

What is not deleted

Nothing linked to your account is kept. The following are not linked to your account or email and are deleted on their own schedule:

  • Anonymous usage statistics: deleted automatically after at most 14 months.
  • Crash reports, which contain no personal data: deleted after at most 90 days.

Afterwards you can also remove Zomnia from your Google Account (Security → Your connections to third-party apps & services) or your Apple Account (Sign in with Apple).
