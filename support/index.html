Questions

Where is my progress saved?

On your phone. If the phone is lost or replaced, or the game is deleted, your progress goes with it. To keep it safe, open Settings → Account and choose Sign in with Google (Android and iOS) or Sign in with Apple (iOS). Your game is then kept in the cloud and updated as you play.

I have a new phone. How do I get my game back?

Install Zomnia on the new phone, open Settings → Account and sign in with the same Google or Apple account. If both the cloud and the new phone have a game, Zomnia asks which one to keep; the other is deleted.

Why can’t I keep playing today?

Zomnia is played one day at a time: each real day brings one day in the game. When your day is over, the main menu shows how long until the next one.

What happens when I die?

Someone new arrives. The map you explored and half of the supplies in your shelter are theirs, so your world goes on.

Do I have to watch ads?

No. Zomnia has no forced ads. In some moments, for example when you are bleeding with no bandage or when you faint, the game offers a rewarded ad. You watch it only if you want what it gives you.

How do I change my ad and privacy choices?

Where the law requires it (for example in the EU and the UK), open Settings → Ad privacy settings. On iOS you can also allow or refuse tracking in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I delete my account?

In Settings → Account, tap Delete account and then tap again to confirm. Your account and cloud save are deleted at once. No longer have the game? See Delete your account.

Which languages does Zomnia speak?

English, Turkish, Spanish, Portuguese (Brazil), German, French, Russian, Indonesian, Japanese, Italian, Polish, Vietnamese, Thai, Korean, Chinese (Simplified and Traditional), Ukrainian, Arabic and Hindi. Change it in Settings → Language.

The game crashed. Do I need to report it?

Crash reports reach us automatically, without any personal data. If it keeps happening, email us anyway with what you were doing: it helps us find the cause faster.
